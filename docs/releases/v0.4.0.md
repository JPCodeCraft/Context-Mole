# Context Mole v0.4.0

Context Mole v0.4.0 keeps related PDF text together in passages, makes each project's status readable at a glance, and keeps search working while a project is indexing.

## One full reindex after updating

This release changes how documents are extracted and split into passages (preparation `layout-v6/spans-v4/body-context-v5`). After the update, every document in every project is re-extracted once in the background, **including OCR of scanned pages and images**, the next time its folder is scanned. Large projects, and projects with many scans, can take as long as their first indexing did.

You don't need to do anything. Existing evidence stays searchable until each document's replacement is committed, paused projects wait for Resume, and source files are never changed. Keep the source folders available so the reindex can finish. There is no database schema change in this release.

## Better passages from PDFs

- PDF layout blocks, OCR blocks and HTML, Markdown, EPUB and email-HTML blocks now merge with their neighbours on the same page and under the same heading while one side is a short fragment (32 words or fewer), up to 256 words. Full paragraphs keep their own passages. On 13 sample PDFs this cut passages from 701 to 251 and passages under three words from 361 to 16.
- Tables keep their column labels, including labels wrapped over two lines, and a "Table N" caption above a table stays with it. Meaning-based search now sees each value with its column name, and long tables are cut only between rows.
- PDF headings are detected from the whole document's font sizes, bold text and section numbering, so passages sit under the right section.
- Prose chunks end and overlap at sentence boundaries. Passages without real words, and OCR text below 40% confidence, stay searchable by keyword but are left out of meaning-based search.
- In hybrid search, a file's previews follow the combined score, so long passages that repeat common query words no longer push out the passage that made the file match.

On the full ViDoRe v3 HR benchmark (1,110 pages, 636 English and Portuguese questions), meaning-based ranking is level with v0.3.1 (Recall@10 0.482 to 0.491), the returned evidence covers more of the right page regions (+0.077), the index holds about 3 times fewer passages, and median search time dropped from 1.2 s to 0.5 s.

## Clearer app

- Each project has one status card: "Indexing · 10 files left", "Everything is indexed" or "Paused · 4 files waiting", what is searchable, a bar of up-to-date, in-progress, waiting and problem files, and the file being indexed now. Timings are under **Show details**.
- A **Search with your AI assistant** card shows keyword search, meaning-based search and connected assistants, with a starter question to copy.
- **Reindex project** and **Remove project** moved into the project's **More** menu. **Files that need attention** has one search box, one filter and one pager.
- Sidebar projects show a status dot and one line, and reorder through a context menu or drag.
- Settings lists AI assistants first and folds the others under **Connect another assistant**. CPU profiles are three cards with their limits.

## Search while indexing

- Searches, continuation pages, `read_passages` and `read_section` no longer fail with `index_changed` while a project is indexing. Evidence is checked per passage: unchanged passages come back exactly as searched, a passage whose document changed comes back as `stale_passage`, and `index_changed_since_search` says whether anything else changed.
- During indexing the broker may reuse a meaning-based vector snapshot from the last 30 seconds and reports `semantic_snapshot_recent` when it does.

## Reliability and housekeeping

- Before a future schema upgrade, the index is copied to `backups/` beside `index.db`. Only the newest copy is kept, for up to 30 days, and it is skipped when free space is short.
- Very large files wait for memory as well as processor capacity, so parallel workers can't each hold a 1 GB file in memory.
- The MCP broker checks who owns its pipe, drops stalled clients after 10 seconds, and keeps indexer traffic off the slots that AI clients use.
- Attachment copies made for an AI client are deleted 24 hours after their last use. Only the 3 newest routine backups of each AI client's settings are kept. Old finished indexing jobs and runs are pruned.
- The app polls less while its window is hidden in the tray.
- `System.Security.Cryptography.Pkcs` is updated to 10.0.14 for GHSA-846f-f764-76mx and GHSA-2fmv-rv2c-59x7.

## Install and update

Download [JPCodeCraft.ContextMole-win-x64-Setup.exe](https://github.com/JPCodeCraft/Context-Mole/releases/download/v0.4.0/JPCodeCraft.ContextMole-win-x64-Setup.exe) for Windows 10+ x64. Existing installed Windows builds can obtain this release through their normal update check. Reload your AI clients' MCP connection after updating.

Windows releases remain unsigned, so SmartScreen may show an unrecognized-app warning. Native Linux and macOS packages are not included.
